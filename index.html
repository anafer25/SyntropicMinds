The Physics of Growth: What is Syntropy?

In 1941, Italian mathematician Luigi Fantappiè was analyzing the core equations of quantum mechanics and relativity when he noticed something incredible. The math didn't just predict entropy (the tendency for things to break down and fade away). It also predicted a mirrored, matching force: syntropy, the mathematical tendency for energy to converge, create order, and build complexity. Derived from the Greek words meaning "to tend toward unity," syntropy is the invisible architecture that pulls raw potential into structured harmony. It is the physics of life, learning, and evolution.
